Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 11, 2026

Lorex ("we", "our", or "the app") is a macOS application that captures your clipboard content and lets you search and chat with it. This policy explains what data is collected, where it goes, and how it is used.

1. Data We Collect

Lorex collects only the data necessary to provide its core functionality:

  • Clipboard content. Text you copy on your Mac is captured and stored locally in an encrypted SQLite database on your device. Lorex never captures passwords, credit card numbers, IBAN numbers, national ID numbers, or API keys unless you explicitly enable those categories in Settings.
  • Chat messages. Messages you type in the chat interface, along with relevant clipboard snippets retrieved from your local database, are sent to our API to generate a response. These are not stored on our servers beyond the duration of the request.

We do not collect your name, email address, or any other personal identifier. We do not use analytics SDKs or advertising frameworks.

2. How Data Is Processed

To power search and chat, Lorex uses two external services:

  • Voyage AI (voyageai.com) — clipboard snippets are sent to Voyage AI's API to generate vector embeddings, which enable semantic search. Voyage AI's privacy policy is available at voyageai.com/privacy.
  • Anthropic Claude — your chat messages and relevant clipboard context are forwarded to Anthropic's Claude API via a secure proxy to generate responses. Anthropic's privacy policy is available at anthropic.com/privacy.

Both services receive only the minimum text necessary to answer your query. No data is sold or shared with any other third party.

3. Data Storage

All captured clipboard content is stored locally on your Mac in the app's sandboxed container. You can delete all stored data at any time from the Captures tab inside the app. Uninstalling Lorex removes all local data.

4. Subscriptions & Payments

Lorex offers optional auto-renewable subscriptions (monthly and yearly) that are processed entirely through Apple's App Store. We never see or store your payment information. Subscription management, cancellation, and refund requests are handled through your Apple ID account settings or at apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions.

5. Children's Privacy

Lorex is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
